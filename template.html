Comparison notes

    Metric definitions and limitations

    Token rate is provider-reported completion tokens divided by total request time, which includes queueing and connection setup and may include reasoning tokens. It is not pure decoder speed. Usage is never inferred from chunk counts. Percentiles use linear interpolation over successful runs. Small samples do not establish provider reliability. Prices are user-supplied assumptions, not a live price feed. Error counts include failed attempts, while measured cost only covers successful requests with reported input and output usage; failed attempts may still be billed.
